The Complete Europull Lifting & Rigging Portfolio

Europull is globally recognized for manufacturing reliable, fail-safe material handling gear designed for rigorous daily use. To ensure seamless integration into your site operations, our inventory covers 11 distinct categories of lifting, pulling, positioning, and securing equipment.

1 Manual Chain Blocks

Engineered for vertical lifting of heavy industrial components with minimal operator effort, manual chain blocks are the backbone of remote and offline work sites. Available in capacities from 0.5T to 30T, they are the standard choice for machinery installation, equipment maintenance, and steel erection where electrical power is unavailable.

Unlike standard chain blocks, lever hoists utilize a ratcheting handle, allowing operators to lift, tension, or pull loads horizontally and at specific angles. 6 Beam Clamps

Beam clamps create secure, temporary rigging attachment points on suitable structural steel beams. With a jaw design that distributes the load away from the beam edge, they allow maintenance teams to quickly set up safe anchor points for hoists during plant shutdowns without permanently modifying the structure.

Handling steel plates safely requires specialized grip mechanisms. Europull plate lifting clamps feature articulated lifting shackles and hardened steel jaws that lock securely onto steel sheets, preventing slippage. They are essential equipment for steel fabrication yards, shipbuilding facilities, and heavy structural manufacturing.

Specifically engineered for the cylindrical shape of industrial pipes, these clamps provide controlled handling and prevent hazardous load rolling. 9 Rigging Shackles (Bow & D-Type)

Shackles serve as the critical connection points in any rigging assembly. Europull’s high-strength, forged alloy bow and D-shackles are used to securely link wire ropes, alloy chains, lifting slings, and hoists. Each shackle is stamped with its specific Working Load Limit (WLL) to ensure load stability during complex multi-point lifts.
