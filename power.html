Power

Reliable power plant maintenance in Saudi Arabia is essential to ensure continuous and safe energy production. Onshore Technical Supplies provides high-quality tools, lifting equipment, and safety solutions designed to support efficient maintenance operations across power plants and energy facilities.

In the power industry, precision and safety are essential. At Onshore Technical Supplies Est, we supply tools, safety gear and lifting solutions for both traditional and renewable energy sectors. From high-voltage electrical work to heavy mechanical maintenance, we provide equipment that helps keep energy flowing without interruption.

We serve the power sector by:

  • Offering trusted solutions for both scheduled operations and emergency response
