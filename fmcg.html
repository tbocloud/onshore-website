FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods)

We supply the right tools to keep things running smoothly and avoid delays.

At Onshore Technical Supplies Est, we help manufacturers, packagers and distributors in the FMCG sector keep operations running smoothly, without costly delays or safety risks. Our decade long experience working with some of the most demanding production environments means we understand the urgency and precision this industry requires

We supply tools and safety equipment made for speed, reliability and compliance. Whether you’re manufacturing food, beverages, personal care items or household goods, our solutions meet strict hygiene and quality control standards. We ensure that every product we deliver is ready to perform in a clean and often high pressure environment.

Here’s how we help FMCG companies stay ahead:

  • Supplying good performance tools and expendable that keep production lines moving without interruption
  • Providing safety gear that meets food grade and hygiene regulations for manufacturing environments
  • Offering fast, reliable delivery to prevent downtime and missed deadlines

We also work closely with maintenance teams, providing rapid replacements for worn or damaged equipment and helping managers plan for preventive maintenance to reduce unexpected stoppages. With our best solutions, FMCG companies can focus on meeting consumer demand while maintaining the highest quality standards

FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What equipment is used in the FMCG industry?

The FMCG industry uses high-speed packaging tools, maintenance equipment, and safety PPE designed for hygiene and efficient production.

2. Do you supply FMCG equipment in Saudi Arabia?

Yes, Onshore Technical Supplies provides industrial tools and safety gear tailored for FMCG manufacturers and distributors across Saudi Arabia.

3. What makes equipment suitable for the food and beverage industry?

Equipment must meet strict hygiene and quality control standards to ensure safety and compliance in food and beverage production environments.

4. Do you provide safety gear for FMCG production workers?

Yes, we supply protective gear such as gloves, helmets, and specialized clothing that meet hygiene regulations for production workers.

5. What is the role of lifting equipment in food manufacturing?

Lifting equipment helps in the safe handling of heavy raw materials and finished products, reducing the risk of workplace injuries.
