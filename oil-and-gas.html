FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What equipment is used in the oil and gas industry in Saudi Arabia?

Oil and gas operations in Saudi Arabia require high-performance welding equipment, heavy-duty lifting tools, and certified safety PPE for pipelines, refineries, offshore rigs, and maintenance activities.

2. Do you supply oilfield equipment in Saudi Arabia?

Yes, Onshore Technical Supplies provides a wide range of industrial equipment, including welding machines, lifting gear, and safety products specifically designed for oilfield and offshore operations.

3. What safety equipment is required for oil & gas projects?

Oil and gas projects require PPE such as flame-resistant clothing, helmets, gloves, safety boots, and respiratory protection to ensure worker safety in hazardous environments.

4. Why is welding important in oil & gas operations?

Welding is critical in oil and gas operations as it ensures strong, leak-proof pipeline joints and structural integrity for safe transportation and processing of materials.

5. Do you provide lifting equipment for oilfield projects?

Yes, we supply heavy-duty lifting equipment such as chain slings, hoists, and rigging tools that are essential for safe handling of materials in oilfield projects.

6. What industries use oil & gas equipment suppliers in KSA?

Oil refineries, drilling contractors, pipeline companies, and offshore operators rely on industrial equipment suppliers for safe and efficient operations.

7. How to choose industrial equipment for oil & gas?

Choose equipment that meets international safety standards, offers durability in harsh conditions, and is suitable for high-pressure and high-temperature environments.

8. Who is a reliable oil & gas equipment supplier in Saudi Arabia?

Onshore Technical Supplies is a trusted supplier known for providing high-quality welding, lifting, and safety equipment for oil and gas industries across Saudi Arabia.
