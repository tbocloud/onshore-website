FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What equipment is required for mining operations?

Mining operations require heavy-duty welding tools, lifting equipment, and durable safety PPE designed to withstand harsh conditions like dust, moisture, and vibration.

2. Do you supply mining equipment in Saudi Arabia?

Yes, Onshore Technical Supplies provides industrial-grade equipment suitable for underground and open-pit mining operations across Saudi Arabia.

3. What PPE is needed for mining?

Mining PPE includes helmets, gloves, boots, eye protection, and respiratory gear to protect workers from hazardous conditions and airborne particles.

4. Why is safety important in mining?

Safety is critical in mining due to high-risk environments, and proper equipment helps prevent accidents, injuries, and long-term health issues.

5. Do you provide lifting equipment for mining?

Yes, we supply heavy-duty lifting tools such as chain slings and hoists that are essential for handling materials safely in mining operations.

6. What makes mining equipment durable?

Mining equipment is designed to resist extreme conditions, including heavy loads, dust, corrosion, and continuous usage in challenging environments.
