FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What does an industrial equipment supplier provide?

An industrial equipment supplier provides welding machines, lifting tools, safety PPE, and related accessories that support operations across construction, manufacturing, oil & gas, and other industrial sectors.

2. What types of welding equipment are available for industrial use?

Industrial welding equipment includes MIG, TIG, and arc welding machines, along with electrodes, cables, and accessories designed for high-performance and precision work.

3. What is the purpose of lifting equipment in industrial operations?

Lifting equipment is used to safely handle and move heavy materials, improving efficiency while reducing the risk of workplace injuries and equipment damage.

4. Why is safety equipment important in industrial environments?

Safety equipment protects workers from hazards such as heat, sparks, chemicals, and heavy loads, helping reduce accidents and maintain a safe working environment.

5. What types of safety PPE are commonly used in industries?

Common PPE includes helmets, gloves, safety shoes, protective clothing, eye protection, and respiratory gear designed to safeguard workers in various industrial conditions.

6. Can one supplier provide solutions for multiple industries?

Yes, experienced suppliers like Onshore Technical Supplies offer solutions tailored to different industries with a wide product range.

7. How can I get support for selecting the right equipment?

You can consult with experienced suppliers who understand industrial requirements and can recommend suitable equipment based on your application needs.
