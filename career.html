Sales Executive (Outdoor)

Head Office – KSA Sales Dammam · Riyadh · Jeddah Full-Time

The Sales Executive drives outdoor B2B sales by prospecting new clients, maintaining existing accounts, and closing deals across the assigned KSA territory — working against a clear monthly target of 350,000 SAR.

🎯 Monthly Target: 350,000 SAR

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct 6–8 customer visits daily; develop new end-user accounts monthly
  • Prepare and follow up on quotations, negotiations, and order closures
  • Track sales activities and pipeline via ERP and Excel reports
  • Ensure timely payment collection within approved credit terms
  • Represent the brand at exhibitions and deliver product demonstrations

Requirements at a Glance

  • Degree in Arts / Management / Engineering or equivalent Diploma
  • 3+ years of outdoor B2B sales experience
  • Strong negotiation, market intelligence, and CRM/ERP skills
  • Valid KSA driving licence — essential for daily field visits

Welding Demonstrator / Service Technician

Head Office – KSA Operations Dammam · Riyadh · Jeddah Full-Time

Onshore Technical Supplies is seeking a skilled Welding Demonstrator to support our sales team by conducting live demonstrations of ARC, TIG, and MIG welding machines for potential customers — while also providing basic troubleshooting and service support.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct live welding machine demonstrations for customers

Requirements at a Glance

  • 3+ years in welding machine operation, demonstration, or service

Procurement Coordinator / Buyer

Head Office – KSA Operations & Supply Chain All Business Units Full-Time

The Procurement Coordinator is responsible for managing contracts, vendor relationships, and inventory control across all business units — ensuring uninterrupted supply flow, cost-effective purchasing, and accurate ERP reporting.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage purchase orders, MRP checks, and vendor evaluations

  • Degree in Supply Chain, Inventory / Materials Management or equivalent
